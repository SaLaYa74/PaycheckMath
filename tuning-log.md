## 2026-10-26 (Mon, full review)

**Numbers (last 7 days):** 4 followers (no change). 6 Reels, every one reached about 105-114 people
except the one posted tonight (too new to judge). Saves, shares and follows: 0 across the board.
Average watch time was 1.7-3.2 s on Reels 4.8-6.8 s long (people watch about half).
Midday and evening reach are the same once tonight's Reel is left out, so posting times stay as they are.

**Changed:**
1. **4 more price trend Reels (bacon, tomatoes, flour, natural gas).** The monthly rotation had only 2
   September formats left (milk, bread). After Wednesday, the 5 weekly rotation slots would have gone
   quiet until October CPI comes out in mid-November. Trend Reels reach the same ~105-114 people as
   everything else, so more of them is a safe bet. All four come from the same official BLS
   average-price data and pass the same blocking checks: freshness, a plausible range for each item,
   a realistic month-over-month move, and an exact year-ago month.

   bacon $7.29 → $6.76 (−7%), natural gas $1.61 → $1.70/therm (+5%), tomatoes $1.91 → $1.99 (+4%),
   flour $0.56 → $0.55 (−3%). Also tested navel oranges and dropped them: BLS skips some summer months,
   so the chart's timeline came out uneven.
2. **No more "up 0%" Reels.** A trend Reel now skips itself if the move rounds to 0%. The headline would
   be meaningless.
3. **Weekly report labels.** Rotation Reels were labeled by weekday ("monthly_tue"), which made it
   impossible to compare formats. They're now labeled by format (trend line, price drops, jobs report,
   find your state, release day).

**Watch next time:** do the new trend Reels hold ~100+ reach? First "Find Your State" Reel (Sat 5pm):
does it get shares? (It's the first post built to be sent to friends.) Shares, saves and follows are
still all zero, and that's the real problem to work on once there are more posts to compare.
